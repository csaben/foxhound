foxhound-helper - drive one native app window without taking the user's mouse or keyboard

Usage:
  foxhound-helper --process APP.exe [--bind 127.0.0.1] [--port 8770] [--token TOKEN]
  foxhound-helper --pid PID ...
  foxhound-helper --title TEXT ...
  foxhound-helper --hwnd N ...

The helper binds to localhost by default. A non-localhost bind requires --token.
